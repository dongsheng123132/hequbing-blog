Support

HackerPodcasts · Last updated November 4, 2026
Start here: pick a flight pack → tap "Download all" while on Wi-Fi before takeoff → switch to airplane mode and listen.
Still stuck? Email support@hequbing.com with your device model, iOS version, and a screenshot. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I listen offline?

Open any flight pack and tap "Download all". Wait until every episode shows a checkmark. Once downloaded, episodes play in airplane mode. Files are stored on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Why are the 6-hour and 12-hour packs locked?

The 3-hour pack (7 episodes) is completely free. The 6-hour pack (10 episodes) and 12-hour pack (12 episodes) unlock with a single $1.99 in-app purchase, including all offline downloads. It is not a subscription and does not auto-renew.

New phone or reinstall — how do I restore?

Use the same Apple ID: open the app → Library → Restore purchases. Apple keeps the purchase record; we never see your payment details.

An episode fails to download or play

Audio is served by the podcast publisher's server and occasionally times out or goes offline. Check your connection and tap retry. If it keeps failing, email us the episode title and we will update the list. Downloaded files are not affected.

Why doesn't the app include audio files?

Audio comes from each podcast's official RSS feed or direct audio link and is downloaded by your device. We do not host or resell audio. Every entry keeps its original RSS and audio URL so you can verify it. Tap "View original HN post" to see the comment that recommended it.

Refunds

In-app purchases are handled by Apple. For a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com and select the purchase. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Content suggestions / copyright

If you want to recommend an episode, or you are a rights holder and believe a link is inappropriate, email support@hequbing.com. We will review and remove the link if needed.

Privacy

See the privacy policy: no account, no tracking, no ads; downloads stay on your device.
