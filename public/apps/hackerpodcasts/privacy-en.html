Privacy Policy

HackerPodcasts · Effective November 4, 2026
In one sentence: this app has no account, collects no personal data, has no ads and no cross-app tracking. It does one thing: downloads public podcast audio to your own device for offline listening.

1. What we do not collect

HackerPodcasts does not require registration or login. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or usage behavior. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Data stored only on your device

  • Podcast audio files you choose to download (stored in the app's local directory);
  • A download index (which episodes are downloaded and their file sizes) so the list can be restored at launch;
  • In-app purchase status is provided by Apple StoreKit. We never see your payment details.

This data is not uploaded to our servers. Deleting the app deletes the local data.

3. Network access

The app contacts a podcast publisher's official RSS feed or direct audio URL only when you choose to play or download an episode. Audio is requested directly by your device from the publisher. We do not host, mirror, or redistribute audio files.

4. In-app purchase

The $1.99 "Unlock all flight packs" purchase is an Apple in-app purchase (non-consumable). Payment, receipts, and refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and media services terms. You can restore a previous purchase in the app with "Restore purchases."

5. Third-party links

Each episode includes a link to the original Hacker News thread and to the podcast page. Opening a link leaves the app and takes you to that website, which has its own privacy policy.

6. Children

The app is not designed for children and does not collect age information. Some podcast episodes may contain mature themes. The App Store age rating is 12+.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. For privacy questions, contact support@hequbing.com.
